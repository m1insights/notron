## What landed (one commit each)

1. **Keychain hold** (`credentials.KeychainStore`): every encrypted read asked the
   helper for the storage key — 89 processes in one idle tick, 2.15 s of 3.6 s.
   Held 30 s; misses and failures never held; put/delete drop the hold.
2. **Bulk note metadata** (`notes.get_notes`): Notes resolves `note id` once per
   property (~0.7 s a lookup) but lists every note in 1.6 s, identically. The
   executor's readability checks use it from 3 notes up; `index.search` answers
   its per-hit checks from the listing its own reconcile just took, never an
   older one. Fresh checks stay fresh.
3. **Settle by Notes' clock** (`watch._settled(quiet=…)`, `Watcher.pause`): on
   first sight one 0.2 s read of the modification date; a line already still for
   the settle window is finished now. Future stamps and failures fall back to the
   second look. The loop wakes when the earliest settling line is due.
4. **Leaner lookup** (`notes._METADATA`): no separate `exists` (0.81 → 0.69 s).
5. **Router and scheduler on Super** — faster and more accurate (table above);
   matches the README's own rule that decisions a person waits on go to Super.
6. **Watcher reuses the request's listing** (`executor.recall`, `remember_all`)
   and **"?" settles in 4 s**.
7. **Idle CPU**: the ledger's crash-leftover sweep once a minute (always on a
   migration); the index/undo purge skipped when policy and files are unchanged.
